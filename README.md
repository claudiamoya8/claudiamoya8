## Research experience

Through an ESIMAIR research scholarship, I worked on the **LLM Syllogisms** research line, evaluating the logical-reasoning capabilities of Llama and Qwen models.

My work included dataset preparation and validation with FOLIO, FOLIO-Easy and RuleTaker; Python-based evaluation tools; experimentation; and analysis of model behaviour and prediction bias.
